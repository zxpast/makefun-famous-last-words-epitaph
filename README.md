One line of famous last words, a tombstone epitaph:

> I asked for my ashes in a coffee tin. They used decaf.

A dying wish in the first person. The twist lands on the last three words: the wish was honoured in the letter and insulted in the spirit — not just any coffee tin, the joyless decaf one. Three concrete nouns collide (ashes, coffee tin, decaf); the narrator is the butt of his own joke; nothing is explained.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes exactly that line (54 characters, no trailing newline) to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
